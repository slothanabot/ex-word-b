## The idea

The breakup text did not say "it's not you, it's me". It said the
relationship had been a placeholder — something to hold the spot until
she figured out what she actually wanted. The word was meant to shrink
two years down to a waiting room, and that is exactly why it aged badly:
it turned out to be the most honest summary of the whole thing.

So the word my ex would use to describe me is **placeholder**. It is
funny at once, it stings because it is true, and it lands with no
explanation needed. Everyone has either been the placeholder or kept one
around while they decided. No near synonym lands the same — "temporary"
is a schedule, "practice" is a rehearsal, but "placeholder" is a person
held in reserve.

## How it works

`main.py` is deterministic: no input, no randomness, no clock, no
network. It writes exactly the word `placeholder` — eleven characters,
no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt   # placeholder
```

## Gate compliance

The sealed scorer requires `/output/entry.txt` to be UTF-8 text of
1–24 characters, not only whitespace. This entry is 11 characters: one
word, nothing else.
